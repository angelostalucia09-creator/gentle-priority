🌞 ANXIETY

Understanding Anxiety

Anxiety is more than just feeling worried. It's when that feeling doesn't go away and starts affecting your daily life. Remember — having anxiety doesn't mean something is wrong with you; it just means your nervous system is working a little too hard.

What Helps in the Moment:

  • 👀 Look around and name 5 things you can see
  • ✋ Name 4 things you can touch
  • 👂 Name 3 things you can hear
  • 👃 Name 2 things you can smell
  • 👅 Name 1 thing you can taste

Daily Practices:

  • ☀️ Start your day with one deep breath and a gentle affirmation
  • 📱 Limit time on social media if it makes you feel worse
  • 🌙 Create a calming bedtime routine
  • 💧 Stay hydrated — it really helps!
  • 🧘 Be patient with yourself — healing takes time

You are not your anxiety. You are the one experiencing it, and you are stronger than you know. 💙
