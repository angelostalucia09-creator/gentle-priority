🌷 DEPRESSION

You Are Not Alone

Depression is more than sadness. It can feel like carrying a heavy weight that no one else sees. But please remember — feeling this way doesn't mean you're broken. It means you've been strong for too long, and it's okay to rest.

Small Steps Are Still Progress:

  • ☀️ Open the curtains and let some light in
  • 💧 Drink a glass of water
  • 🛏️ If you can't get out of bed, that's okay too
  • 🍎 Eat something — even just a little
  • 📞 Reach out to one person — you don't have to explain, just say "I'm having a hard day"

Important Reminders:

  • 🌅 Your feelings are temporary — this will pass
  • 💪 You don't have to fight it — it's okay to feel it
  • 🆘 It's okay to ask for professional help — that takes courage
  • 💚 You matter. Your life has value. The world needs you

Even the darkest night ends and the sun rises again. Tomorrow is a fresh start. 🕊️
