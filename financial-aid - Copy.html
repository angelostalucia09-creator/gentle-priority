💰 Financial Assistance — Philippines

Organizations and agencies that can help with medical, hospitalization, and emergency expenses

🏛️ Government Agencies

DSWD — Department of Social Welfare and Development

Program: Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)

What they help with: Medical bills, medicines, hospitalization, emergency financial aid

📞 Hotline: (02) 8931-8101 to 07 | 0918-912-2937

📍 In Cebu: Visit your City/Municipal Social Welfare Office (CSWDO) in your local City Hall

🌐 www.dswd.gov.ph →

Malasakit Center

What it is: One-stop shop inside government hospitals — combines aid from DSWD, PhilHealth, PCSO, and PAGCOR

What they help with: Hospital bills, medicines, lab tests, medical procedures

📍 In Cebu: Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center | Cebu City Medical Center | Cebu Provincial Hospital (Talisay)

🌐 malasakitcenter.ph →

PCSO — Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office

Program: Medical Assistance Program

What they help with: Hospital bills, medicines, lab tests, dialysis, cancer treatment

📞 Hotline: (02) 8524-9071 to 79

📍 In Cebu: Ground Floor, Mandaue City Hall Bldg, Mandaue City | (032) 346-1684

🌐 www.pcso.gov.ph →

PhilHealth

What it is: National health insurance — indigent members may be sponsored by the LGU for free

What they help with: Hospitalization, mental health confinement, medical procedures

📞 Hotline: 0917-888-7444 | 0998-888-7444

🌐 www.philhealth.gov.ph →

🤝 NGOs & Community Organizations

Philippine Red Cross

What they help with: Emergency medical assistance, ambulance services, blood services, disaster relief

📞 National Hotline: 143 | (02) 8527-0000

📍 Cebu Chapter: 78 Osmeña Boulevard, Cebu City | (032) 253-5888

🌐 www.redcross.org.ph →

Caritas Cebu

What they help with: Medical assistance, emergency aid, social services for those in need

📍 Location: 26 D. Jakosalem Street, Cebu City

📞 Phone: (032) 254-9581

Walk in or call first — bring hospital papers and ID when applying.

📝 What to Prepare When Applying

Most agencies will ask for: 1) Hospital bill or medical certificate, 2) Valid ID of patient and applicant, 3) Barangay certificate of indigency, 4) Quotation from the hospital or pharmacy

ℹ️ Important Reminder

Contact each agency directly for their specific requirements and schedule. Some may require you to visit in person. Assistance programs may change — call ahead before going. In case of emergency, go to your nearest government hospital.
