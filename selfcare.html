🌿 SELF CARE / SUPPORT

Self Care Isn't Selfish

Taking care of yourself is one of the most important things you can do. You can't pour from an empty cup — take care of yourself first.

Daily Self-Care Ideas:

  • 🌅 Morning: Start with gratitude — name 3 things you're thankful for
  • 🌿 Daytime: Take 5 minutes to step outside and breathe fresh air
  • 🌙 Evening: Disconnect from screens 30 minutes before sleeping
  • 💧 Drink water first thing in the morning
  • 🧹 Declutter one small space — it clears your mind too

How to Support Others:

  • 👂 Listen without fixing — sometimes people just need to be heard
  • 💙 Validate their feelings: "That sounds really hard"
  • 🤝 Offer simple help: "Would you like some company?"
  • 💌 Check in on them — a simple "I'm thinking of you" can change someone's day
  • 🙏 Remind them: "You don't have to go through this alone"

Self-care is how you take your power back. Be kind to yourself today. ✨
