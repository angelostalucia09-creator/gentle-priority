🌿 STRESS

Understanding Stress

Stress is a natural human response to challenges and demands. When you feel threatened or under pressure, your body releases stress hormones that prepare you to face the situation.

Common Signs of Stress:

  • Feeling overwhelmed or constantly worried
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Sleep problems
  • Physical tension or headaches
  • Changes in appetite

Healthy Ways to Manage Stress:

  • 🌱 Take deep breaths — try the 4-7-8 breathing technique
  • 🚶 Move your body — even a short walk helps
  • 📝 Write down what's bothering you
  • 🛌 Get enough rest
  • 💬 Talk to someone you trust
  • 🎨 Make time for things you enjoy

Remember: It's okay to take a break. Your worth isn't measured by how much you accomplish each day. 💚
